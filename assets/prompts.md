## Shared direction

Landscape 4:3 mathematical teaching diagrams for adults with little mathematical training. Restrained Victorian scientific-plate styling: warm ivory paper with extremely faint texture, precise charcoal ink, muted teal emphasis, copper-orange contrast, minimal antique-brass framing. Flat geometry, generous space, large clean labels and restrained serif headings. Steampunk character comes through craftsmanship. No people, portraits, gears, physical machinery, decorative equations, invented data or extra text. Preserve mathematical counts and relationships; peripheral ornament must never look like mathematical content.
